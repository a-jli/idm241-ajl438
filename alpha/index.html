Loops & Modes

Star Icon

  • Stars change from Unclicked to Clicked, with fill and outline color changing
    • Once any Star is clicked, the hover colors change until the "Rate & Review" popup is closed.
    • Unclicked hover colors are WHITE (fill), GREY (outline) and DARK GREY (fill, outline)
    • Clicked hover colors are WHITE (fill) and RED (fill, outline)
  • Once any Star Icon is clicked, the leftmost Star cannot be clicked off and will stay filled in RED, for a mininum 1 star rating
